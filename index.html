Transport playbook

Manchester Airport → Liverpool · 18 Dec

After immigration, take a direct train to Liverpool Lime Street if the live timetable is suitable. Because flight arrival time is uncertain, use a flexible ticket rather than a restricted Advance ticket. Allow about 75–90 minutes.

National Rail planner ↗
Liverpool city → Anfield · 19 Dec

Use the 917 matchday express from Commutation Row near Lime Street. It starts 3½ hours before kick-off; return buses leave from Walton Lane. Contactless payment accepted.

LFC transport guide ↗
Liverpool → Chester · 20 Dec

Merseyrail Wirral Line from Liverpool Central to Chester, roughly 45–50 minutes and normally four trains per hour. No advance reservation needed; buy a day return.

Merseyrail planner ↗
Liverpool → London · 21 Dec

Direct Avanti from Lime Street to Euston, normally around 2¼–2½ hours. From Euston, Pullman London St Pancras is an easy 10–12 minute walk.

Avanti booking ↗
London local travel · 21–24 Dec

Use contactless Visa/Mastercard or Apple Pay. Tap the same device/card in and out. Pullman is beside King’s Cross St Pancras, served by six Underground lines.

TfL journey planner ↗
London → Bruges · 24 Dec

Be at St Pancras Eurostar security by about 11:30 for the 13:04 departure. At Brussels Midi, follow signs to Belgian domestic trains and take a direct train to Brugge; reservations are not needed.

Eurostar check-in advice · SNCB
Bruges → Ghent · 26 Dec

Direct SNCB train from Brugge to Gent-Sint-Pieters, about 25 minutes. Due to city-centre tram works, take tram T1 to Kouter and walk 8–12 minutes to St Bavo/Korenmarkt; check De Lijn live routing that morning.

De Lijn planner ↗
Bruges → Brussels → London → Manchester · 27 Dec

Leave Brugge around 09:00 and aim to reach Brussels Midi by 10:15–10:30. Eurostar 9135 departs 12:56 and arrives St Pancras 13:57 local time. Walk 10–12 minutes to Euston. Use a reserved Avanti service after 15:30.

Manchester hotel → airport · 29 Dec

Walk to Piccadilly station and take a direct train to Manchester Airport (normally about 15–20 minutes). Leave the hotel around 09:15–09:30 for the 13:15 Emirates departure.

Practical notes

Weather & daylight

Expect cold, damp and windy weather, roughly 2–10°C. Sunset is around 15:50–16:00. Bring waterproof shoes, compact umbrellas, gloves and layered outerwear.

Christmas closures

Bruges museums close on 25 Dec. Many London museums close 24–26 Dec, but you leave London on the 24th. Manchester Christmas markets end on 23 Dec, so they will not be operating on 27–28 Dec.

Navigation

Google Maps and Apple Maps work well in the UK and Belgium. Install TfL Go, SNCB/NMBS, De Lijn and Avanti apps before travel; download the itinerary for offline reference.
